APP APPEARANCE

A compact mixing surface: channels own their faders, meters and applications.

INTERACTIVE CONCEPT

Fictional apps, routes and static meters. Gain/mute is proposed Linux send control; no audio or device is accessed. Remote personal/audience faders are not implemented.

THE REFERENCES

Actual interfaces, specific decisions.

01 / CHANNEL OWNERSHIP

BEACN App

Paired faders, thin channel colors, meters and app wells all live in the same strip. Our first study lost that relationship.

Borrow: apps beneath their fader; consistent channel positions. Leave: the many controls for unimplemented hardware/mixes.

02 / SIGNAL RELATIONSHIPS

Elgato Wave Link 3

Sources form rows, mixes form columns, and send controls occupy intersections. Routing is the main view rather than a secondary list.

Borrow: a readable source-to-destination map. Adapt: LinX assignments are observed, so its cells select a source and do not claim to reroute it.

03 / GLANCEABLE CATEGORIES

SteelSeries Sonar

Game, chat and media have clear identities. Tall faders and application labels make the channel relationship easy to recognize.

Borrow: channel identity and a compact mute affordance. Leave: the large suite sidebar and effects navigation.

04 / INSTRUMENT PRECISION

Universal Audio Console

Fader caps, calibrated scales, narrow meters and aligned readouts make this feel like an audio instrument rather than a settings screen.

Borrow: precise fader geometry and numeric alignment. Leave: plugin racks, analog imitation and recording-console density.

Official source ↗
CHOSEN DIRECTION

Console is the default. Mini keeps its controls close when space matters; Signal map remains a layout alternative. All three share Light/Dark themes and five accent palettes. The lynx and connection indicator always retain their actual status colors.
